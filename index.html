ABOUT SKYHARVEST

Innovation with a Purpose.

SkyHarvest is an entrepreneurship venture exploring atmospheric water generation as a potential supplementary water source. Our concept combines intelligent monitoring, moisture extraction, water treatment, and renewable-energy ideas.
