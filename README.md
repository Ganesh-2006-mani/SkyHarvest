
# ☁️ SkyHarvest — Water From Air, A Better Tomorrow

SkyHarvest is an entrepreneurship venture exploring atmospheric water generation using AI-assisted monitoring and solar-assisted technology. The project focuses on extracting moisture from the air, collecting the water, and applying appropriate filtration and purification to investigate alternative approaches to drinking water access.

## 🌍 About the Project

Water is essential for life, and access to safe drinking water remains an important global challenge. SkyHarvest explores how atmospheric moisture and innovative technology could contribute to sustainable water solutions.

Our concept combines:
- Atmospheric moisture extraction
- AI-assisted environmental monitoring
- Humidity and temperature sensors
- Solar-assisted energy
- Water collection, filtration, and purification

**Our vision:** To explore innovative and responsible ways to turn atmospheric moisture into a potential supplementary water source.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore atmospheric water generation technology.
- Investigate how AI-assisted monitoring can support system operation.
- Explore solar energy as a supplementary power source.
- Understand the importance of water filtration, purification, and quality testing.
- Present our venture through a modern, responsive website.
- Encourage collaboration and discussion around sustainable water innovation.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

You only need a modern web browser and a text editor, such as Visual Studio Code.

### Installation

1. Download or clone the project repository.
2. Open the project folder in your preferred code editor.
3. Ensure that `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` are in the same folder.
4. Open `index.html` in your browser.

No package installation or build process is required.
